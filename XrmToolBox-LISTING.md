## Short summary

Compare and safely transfer Word and Excel document templates between Microsoft Dataverse environments.

## Full description

Dynamics 365 Template Compare & Transfer is an XrmToolBox tool for comparing Word and Excel document templates across two Microsoft Dataverse environments. It identifies identical, different, source-only, target-only, and duplicate records; provides dry-run previews; separates missing-template copies from existing-template updates; warns when a target is newer; preserves Draft and Activated status; and verifies every target record after a write.

The tool also includes search and filtering, detailed row diagnostics, normalized package comparison, CSV evidence export, visible activity logging, same-environment blocking, and duplicate protection. It never deletes document templates.
